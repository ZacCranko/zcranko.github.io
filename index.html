Zachary Cranko

San Francisco, California

I build high-performance infrastructure and tooling for machine learning at Google DeepMind. I tend to take hard, slow, or poorly understood systems and rebuild them from scratch so they run fast and get out of the way.

Before machine learning, I worked on film and television scores for US and UK productions, went back to university for economics and a PhD in statistical learning theory, and joined Midjourney as its tenth employee.

Now

At Google DeepMind (2026 – Present), I build high-throughput, low-latency infrastructure and developer tooling operating over heavy daily data volumes, used primarily by the Gemini teams alongside engineers and agents across research and engineering.

Most of my code is in Rust, C++, Python, and Swift, working close to JAX, XLA, and accelerator hardware, with custom GPU-rendered interfaces when standard tools can't keep up.

Papers

My academic work is on the mathematical structure of learning problems, especially convex loss geometry, information–risk equalities, distributional robustness, and differential privacy.
